Review of DESIGN-image-fields.md from @curio. The design reads well and the model is right. Six points, most important first.

1. world_width IS AMBIGUOUS, AND SCALE FROM IT BREAKS FOR SIDE VIEWS (a correction). The doc calls world size intrinsic, never per use, and also derives metres per pixel from world_width/content_w. Those conflict once the view turns. The blimp is '40 m long': seen from the side, the picture's width shows its LENGTH (its z extent), not its width (x). Seen from the front, the same picture width would be about 10 m. world_height has the same problem away from level pitch, from above especially. Two clean ways out: (a) intrinsic size as three numbers in the thing's own frame, world_width (x), world_height (y) and world_depth (z), so the extent seen in any view follows from the view vector; or (b) world size as seen in THIS picture (per image, not intrinsic). (a) keeps 'intrinsic', and a spin's keyframes then need no size changes.

2. WHICH FRAME IS LIGHT IN? (a correction, or at least an ambiguity). Light is stored 'in the same frame' as the view, the thing's frame. But it's decided and composed in the PICTURE's frame: a venue lights everything 'from the upper left' of the picture, and shadows agree when props are put together on screen. In the thing's frame, two props lit identically on screen but seen from different sides get different light vectors, and 'lit from the upper left' stops being a range. Suggest storing light in the picture frame (x right, y up, z toward the viewer). It can still be turned into the thing's frame through the view when needed.

3. SINGLE-BEST TYPES AND VIEWS. A hand-added trait word (facing: left) can contradict the view vector, and nothing checks them against each other. Since a view already has a named direction, which is a WORD derived from the vector, could a registration name it as a key part? Something like pool_traits: %w[thing ... direction fmt], with direction filled by DD from the view rather than by the app. That keeps numbers out of keys (the name is a sector word) and keys in step with fields; editing a view across a sector would re-key, which is rare and honest. And for pooled types: an optional guard against two rows with the same key AND the same named direction (or the same file_hash), because a pool otherwise accepts an accidental duplicate silently.

4. ONE VOCABULARY FOR DIRECTIONS. The search words are front, back, right-side, left-side, from-above, from-below, top-down. The named directions are front, front-right, right, ..., back-left, from-above, from-below, top-down, bottom-up. right-side vs right will trip people up; use one set for both. Also say whether view=back-left matches a back-left picture taken from above (my guess: yes, with from-above as a second, independent word).

5. A WORKED EXAMPLE OF THE SIDE CONVENTION. Intake will get this backwards: a profile whose nose points LEFT in the picture shows the thing's RIGHT side, so yaw = 1 (camera on its right). Worth one line in the doc and in the API docs.
