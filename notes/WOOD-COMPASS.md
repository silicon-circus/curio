# Compass plaque

`compass.dark-wood.png` — a round hardwood disc with a compass rose, 1024×1024 RGBA, corner alpha 0,
shot square-on so the disc is a true circle (circularity 100%, aspect 1.001) rather than an ellipse.

Effectively centred (2px high of frame centre), so a needle or pointer can be rotated about (512,510). Left/right mirror mismatch is 0.1%, so the rose is genuinely symmetric.

## Lettering

N top, E right, S bottom, W left, with NE / SE / SW / NW correctly placed on the diagonals — all
correct on the first attempt. Worth recording, given how badly the same model mangled the stopwatch
sub-dial numerals: **single letters at cardinal positions are reliable where sequences of numbers
are not.** Naming each letter with its position ("the letter N at the TOP, the letter E at the
RIGHT…") rather than listing them as a set is what to repeat.

## Deviation from the brief

Asked for a compass *painted* in worn cream-white on almost-black wood; what came back is **carved
and gilded** into medium walnut. Richer than specified, but not the same thing — and there is no red
north tip. If the painted look is wanted it needs another roll; the incised treatment is what the
model reaches for when "wood" and "compass" appear together.
